About the System

Computer vision-based skin analysis designed to support early awareness and clinical review.

🧠

CV-Based Detection

The system analyzes uploaded skin images using a trained computer vision model to provide a preliminary disease prediction.

📸

Image-Based Review

Users can upload JPG, PNG, or WebP skin images and view both the computer vision prediction and relevant contextual information.

⚕️

Clinical Awareness

Predictions are meant for educational information and should be reviewed by a qualified healthcare professional.

How the project helps

This application supports skin health monitoring by helping users record image history, review computer vision-based predictions, and understand the general importance of confidence, symptoms, and professional check-ups.
